Frequently asked questions

What you ask us

Do I need a building permit?+

The module is a product placed on ground screws, not a conventional building. The placement regime depends on the module size and the specific plot. We go through it with you before signing, it is part of preparing the order.

Can I buy on instalments?+

Yes, through our leasing partner. Indicatively from CZK 13,500 a month with 20% down for the Host. A specific offer comes with your enquiry.

Does it pay off for rentals?+

AirDNA data shows a sauna lifts the nightly rate by about 7%, a hot tub by 15 to 35%, and occupancy rises by up to 10%. At 15 or more nights a month the Host typically pays back in four to six years from the nightly rate alone, longer for less busy properties. The calculator above uses your numbers.

What connections do I need?+

400 V power with a 3× 25 to 32 A breaker depending on the build, water and drain for the shower and hot tub. The plunge tub itself works without a permanent water connection.

Does it work in winter?+

The module is insulated for year-round use and all lines run inside the tempered structure. Winter is the best season for contrast.

How does service work?+

Annual service check of the heater, chiller and filtration, first year included. 5-year warranty on the structure, 2 years on technology.

What if I move?+

The module can be lifted and moved the same way it arrived. It is a natural feature, not a compromise.
