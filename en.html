Do I need a building permit?+

The module is a product set on ground screws, not a conventional building. The placement regime depends on module size and your plot. We go through it with you before signing — it's part of preparing the order.

Can I buy on finance?+

Yes, through our leasing partner. Indicatively from CZK 13,500 a month with 20% down on the Host. We prepare a concrete offer together with your enquiry.

Does it pay off as a rental?+

AirDNA data puts a sauna at around +7% on nightly rate, a hot tub at +15–35%, and occupancy up to 10% higher. The more nights you sell, the faster it pays back. At 15+ nights a month the Host typically lands around four to six years on rate alone; slower at quieter properties. The calculator above runs your numbers.

What utilities do I need?+

400 V power with a 3× 25–32 A breaker depending on the build, plus water and drain for the shower and hot tub. The plunge itself runs without a permanent water connection.

Does it work in winter?+

The module is insulated year-round and services run inside the tempered structure. Winter is the best contrast season.

How does servicing work?+

An annual check of heater, chiller and filtration — first year included. A 5-year warranty on the frame, 2 years on technology.

What if I move house?+

The module lifts and travels the same way it arrived. That's a native property of the product, not a compromise.
